::analogy
Every app is a restaurant: a dining room and a kitchen.
::

The [[frontend]] is the dining room. It's everything you see and touch: the menu, the tables, the
buttons, the layout. It's designed to be pleasant and easy, because that's the part the guest lives in.

::figure id=dining-room-and-kitchen label="Fig. 3 — Two rooms" caption="You sit in one. The work happens in the other."

The [[backend]] is the kitchen. You never walk in, but it's where the real work happens: your order is
cooked, ingredients are stored, the actual meal is made. When you tap "save" and your data is there
next time, that's the kitchen doing its job out of sight.

::note
This is why a beautiful app can still be slow, or a plain one can be rock-solid. Nice dining room, different kitchen.
::

::say-it-back
"Frontend is the dining room. Backend is the kitchen."
::
