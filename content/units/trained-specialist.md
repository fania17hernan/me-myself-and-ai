::analogy
A custom assistant is a specialist you train once.
::

Most of the time you brief a generalist from scratch: "answer in my voice, keep it short, here's the
format." Do that same briefing every single day and you are wasting the best part of the tool. A
[[custom-assistant]] is where you train the helper once and it remembers.

::figure id=trained-specialist label="Fig. 4 — The specialist" caption="The instructions stay pinned. You don't re-explain them."

You set it up with standing instructions, its job, its tone, its rules, maybe a few reference files,
and give it a name. From then on you just call it. The specialist shows up already knowing the account,
the style guide, the steps, because the briefing is pinned to the wall, not repeated out loud.

::note
Good first candidates: the email you rewrite daily, the weekly report, the format you keep re-describing.
::

It is the same underlying AI. The difference is that you stopped starting from zero. Train it once,
then delegate.

::say-it-back
"A custom assistant is a specialist you train once. It shows up ready every time."
::
