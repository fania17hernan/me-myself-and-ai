_A snapshot, not a spec sheet. Model names move fast, so read this with the date stamp in mind and
lean on the shape, which barely changes._

Anthropic makes Claude, the AI this course runs on. Think of their garage in the same terms as
choosing the vehicle: a few [[model]] tiers, named so you can tell the heavy haulers from the bikes.

- **Opus** is the powerful, top-tier line, the moving truck for hard reasoning and long, careful work.
- **Sonnet** is the balanced everyday car: quick, capable, the default for most day-to-day chat.
- **Haiku** is the light, fast, low-cost bike, built for speed and high volume.

As of this check, there's also a newer top tier, **Fable**, sitting above Opus as the most capable
option. The exact version numbers (Opus 4-point-something, Sonnet 5, and so on) shuffle every few
months. The tier names are what stay put.

::note
The names change, the roles don't. Heavy job, reach for Opus or Fable. Quick job, Sonnet or Haiku.
::
