Most people meet AI as a text box and assume that is the whole thing. It isn't. A [[multimodal]] AI
takes in more than words: you can show it a picture, talk to it out loud, or hand it a screenshot, and
it works with all of them.

::figure id=eyes-and-ears label="Fig. 1 — Eyes and ears" caption="Text, image, and voice all feed the same helper."

## Eyes: show it, don't describe it

A photo of the error on your screen, the label on a broken part, a page of handwritten notes, a chart
in a report. Instead of typing a careful description, you show it and ask. Often the picture carries
detail you wouldn't have known to put into words.

## Ears and voice: just talk

You can speak your question and hear the answer read back. Useful when your hands are busy, when you
think out loud better than you type, or when you're walking. Some tools also make images or audio, but
the everyday win is simpler: stop typing when a photo or your voice is faster.
