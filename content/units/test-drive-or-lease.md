The free version is a real test drive. It is the same car, not a crippled toy. You can feel exactly
how it handles before you pay a cent. The paid version is the lease: you keep the keys, and you get
more of everything.

## What the paid tier actually buys

Three things, mostly. More room before you hit a [[rate-limit]], so you are not cut off mid-task.
More speed, so answers come back faster when everyone is online. And more features: the newest and
most capable models, file uploads, image generation, connections to your other tools. The [[plan-tier]]
is the level of access you are paying for, nothing more mysterious than that.

## When the free tier is genuinely enough

If you use it a few times a day for writing, questions, and quick drafts, the test drive may be all
you need. Pay when you hit the limits often enough that the interruptions cost you more than the
subscription does. That is the whole calculation, and it is fine to take a few weeks to feel it out.

::check answer=1
You upgrade from free to paid. What are you mainly getting?
- A different, secret AI you can't reach on the free plan
- The same tool with higher limits, more speed, and extra features
- A guarantee the answers are always correct
::good Right. Same car, better lease terms. It does not make the answers more trustworthy.
