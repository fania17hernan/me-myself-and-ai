_A snapshot, not a spec sheet. Read it with the date stamp; the tier shape outlasts the numbers._

Google makes Gemini, built into Search, Workspace, and Android, so many people use it without going
looking for it. Its fleet is simpler to read than most, sorting into two main [[model]] roles from
choosing the vehicle:

- **Pro** is the powerful tier, the truck for the heaviest reasoning and long, complex work.
- **Flash** is the fast, low-cost tier, the everyday car-and-bike for quick tasks and high volume.

As of this check, the widely available models are in the **Flash** line (with an even cheaper
Flash-Lite for high volume), while the top **Pro** flagship comes and goes in limited release. The
version numbers (3-point-something) move often. Pro-for-power and Flash-for-speed is the part to keep.

::note
Because Gemini is woven into Google's own apps, the model you're using is often chosen for you in the background. Same two roles underneath.
::
