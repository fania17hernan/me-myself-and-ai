Level 4 is for the curious. The moment you start poking, you hit words engineers use, and they can
feel like a locked door. This is the orientation before we open each one. The whole level rests on a
single reassurance.

::figure id=airport label="Fig. 1 — The airport" caption="You read the signs and find your gate. You don't fly the plane."

## You are a traveler, not a pilot

Think of an airport. To catch your flight you read signs, find your gate, follow the flow. You never
enter the cockpit or fix an engine, and no one expects you to. AI is the same building. There are
pilots and mechanics, the people who build the [[model]], and there are travelers, everyone else, who
just need to get somewhere.

## What the rest of this level does

Each unit takes one intimidating word, frontend, API, database, deployment, and hands you the
traveler's version: enough to read the sign and keep moving. You'll still steer with a plain-language
[[prompt]]. You're just learning what the signs mean.
