::analogy
Beside the conversation, the AI pulls up a workbench.
::

Ask most AIs to write a report and the words scroll by inside the chat, where they are awkward to
edit. Ask for an [[artifact]] and something different happens: a workbench opens to the side, and the
AI builds the actual thing on it. A document, a table, a small working app, a chart.

::figure id=workbench label="Fig. 2 — The bench" caption="The chat talks. The bench holds the thing being made."

The point is that the thing on the bench is _live_. You do not copy it out and paste it somewhere
else. You edit it right there, ask for one section to change, and only that section changes. The
conversation and the object it produces finally sit in two separate places.

::note
Tell-tale sign you're looking at an artifact: a panel opens beside the chat and the answer lives in it, not in the message stream.
::

So when the goal is a _thing_ and not just an answer, a draft you'll revise, a page you'll ship, ask
for it on the bench.

::say-it-back
"The chat is the conversation. The workbench is where the thing gets built."
::
