::analogy
A chat is a phone call. A project is an office.
::

A chat is a quick call. You get what you need and hang up. Perfect for a one-off question, and like
any call, when it ends the room goes quiet and nothing is left on a desk. Start again tomorrow and you
are re-explaining from the top.

::figure id=phone-call-and-office label="Fig. 1 — Call vs. office" caption="Same helper, two ways of keeping the work."

A [[project]] is a dedicated office for one job. The files you dropped in, the instructions you gave,
every earlier conversation, all of it stays on the desk. Walk back in next week and the AI already
has the context, because you never cleared the room.

::note
Rule of thumb: a question is a call. Anything you'll return to more than once deserves an office.
::

You still use plain chats all day for quick things. You reach for a project when a task has a life
longer than one sitting: a client account, a book, a launch, a course.

::say-it-back
"A chat is a phone call. A project is an office where the work stays."
::
