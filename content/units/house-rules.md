::analogy
Custom instructions are the house rules on the wall.
::

Every time you start a chat, you probably repeat the same setup: keep it brief, no jargon, call me by
my first name, I work in real estate. [[custom-instructions]] let you write those once, like house
rules posted on the wall, and the AI follows them in every conversation without being told again.

::figure id=house-rules label="Fig. 1 — The rules" caption="Posted once. Applied to every chat, automatically."

They live in a settings box, not in the chat. Good ones are small and durable: who you are, how you
like answers, what to avoid. "Answer in plain language, get to the point, I'm a nurse, avoid long
preambles." From then on, every reply already fits.

::note
Keep them short. A handful of clear rules beats a page the AI has to weigh on every single message.
::

This is the first real step from _using_ a generic tool to _owning_ a setup that sounds like it was
made for you, because it was.

::say-it-back
"Custom instructions are house rules: written once, followed every time."
::
