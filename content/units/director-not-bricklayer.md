::analogy
You're the director. The AI is the bricklayer.
::

You've heard people say they "built an app" without knowing how to code. That's [[vibe-coding]]: you
describe what you want in plain language, "a page where clients can pick a time and it emails me,"
and the AI writes the actual code. You never touch a brick.

::figure id=director-not-bricklayer label="Fig. 2 — Direct, don't lay" caption="You say what to build. It does the laying."

The skill shifts from _knowing how_ to _knowing what_. A good director is clear about the goal, notices
when a wall is crooked, and asks for the fix. You don't need to know how the bricks are made to say
"this door is in the wrong place, move it."

::note
It's real and it's powerful for small tools. For anything important, the "human in the loop" still checks the work, which is a later unit.
::

::say-it-back
"In vibe coding I direct in plain words. The AI lays the bricks."
::
