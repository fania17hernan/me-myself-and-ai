::analogy
Deployment is the day you unlock the door and open the shop.
::

You can build a whole shop inside a warehouse: shelves stocked, lights working, register ready. Until
you move it to a street address and unlock the door, no customer can walk in. [[deployment]] is that
move: taking the app you built and putting it somewhere the public can actually reach it.

::figure id=opening-the-shop label="Fig. 6 — Open" caption="Built inside, then moved to the street and unlocked."

"Hosting" is the address, the rented space on the internet where your shop sits. "Deploying" is the
act of opening it there, and re-deploying is flipping the sign again after you've restocked. It's the
gap between "it works on my laptop" and "anyone can use it."

::note
This course itself was deployed. It was built in files, then opened to the street so you could walk in and read this.
::

::say-it-back
"Deployment is opening the shop: what I built inside is now open to the public."
::
