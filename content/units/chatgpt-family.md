_A snapshot, not a spec sheet. These names turn over fastest of all, so trust the date stamp and the
shape more than any single number._

OpenAI makes ChatGPT, the tool most people met AI through. Its fleet is the GPT-5 line, which fully
replaced the older GPT-4 and GPT-3.5 you may still hear named. Like any [[model]] menu, it sorts into
the same three roles from choosing the vehicle:

- A **flagship** tier for the heaviest reasoning and research-grade work.
- A **balanced** tier that most people use by default, quick but capable.
- A **fast, low-cost** tier for high-volume, lightweight tasks.

As of this check the current wave is GPT-5.6, with a flagship, a balanced, and a fast member, sitting
on top of the earlier GPT-5 models still available underneath. The decimals climb every few months.
What lasts is the pattern: heavy, balanced, fast.

::note
In the app you'll often just see "Auto" pick for you. The tier names matter when you want to force the heavy one for a hard job.
::
