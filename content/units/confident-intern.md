::analogy
It answers like a brilliant intern who will never say "I don't know."
::

Picture the intern everyone has worked with: quick, eager, genuinely sharp, and physically incapable
of admitting a blank. Ask for a number they don't have and they will give you one that _sounds_
exactly right. That is [[confabulation]], and it is the single most important thing to understand
about AI.

::figure id=confident-intern label="Fig. 2 — The intern" caption="The tone is identical whether the fact is real or invented."

The tone never changes. A true answer and a made-up one arrive with the same easy confidence, because
the AI is not looking anything up. It is predicting words that fit, and a plausible wrong answer fits
just as smoothly as a right one.

::note
So the fluency you feel is not evidence. It is the default setting, on every answer.
::

This does not make it useless. It makes it an intern. Brilliant for a first draft, a summary, a
brainstorm. Never the final word on a name, a number, a date, or a quote you can't afford to get wrong.

::say-it-back
"Confidence is not accuracy. Check the facts that matter."
::
