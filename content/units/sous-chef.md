You are the head chef. The AI is your sous chef: fast, tireless, good with a knife, and waiting for
you to call the order. A [[workflow]] is just the recipe you hand it, the same steps in the same order,
every time.

::figure id=sous-chef label="Fig. 3 — The line" caption="You call the order. It does the prep."

## 1 · Name the dish

Start with the finished plate, not the first cut. "A weekly summary email to my team from these
notes" beats "help me with my notes." The clearer the dish, the less the sous chef has to guess.

## 2 · Write the prep steps

Break it into moves you could hand to a new hire: pull the three highlights, draft it in a friendly
tone, keep it under 150 words, end with next steps. Each step is small and unambiguous.

## 3 · Hand them over in order

Give one step, check the result, then the next. This is the whole trick to a [[prompt]] that works:
you are not asking for magic, you are running a line. When the recipe is good, you paste the same
steps next week and get the same dinner.

::note
Try one this week: take a task you do every Friday and write it out as three prep steps. That's your first workflow.
::
